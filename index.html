Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Tioflos NFC cards.

An NFC (Near Field Communication) card is a smart card with a chip inside. When someone taps it with their smartphone, it instantly opens your digital profile — no app needed. It works on all modern Android and iPhone devices.
No app is needed. Any modern smartphone (iPhone 7+ or Android with NFC) can tap the card and open your profile directly in the browser. It's completely free for your customers.
We deliver same-day within Arba Minch city center. After you place your order, we confirm within 1–2 hours and deliver by bike. Orders placed in the evening may be delivered the next morning.
